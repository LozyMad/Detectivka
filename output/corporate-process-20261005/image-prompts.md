## Компьютер

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: production background artwork for the Russian detective game DETECTUM corporate process section.
Primary request: create one exquisitely art-directed cinematic detective dossier collage, a portrait-like landscape canvas approximately 1536 by 1408. It must feel like a premium noir film art book: warm copper light, deep ink black, aged ivory paper, quiet historical city cartography, photographic material detail, convincing restrained layered paper and shadows.
Input image: the referenced night-city detective artwork is STYLE AND PALETTE REFERENCE ONLY. Do not reuse its people, city image or composition.
Composition: front-facing almost flat-lay website illustration, four large blank ivory evidence-paper sheets arranged in a clear descending zigzag. Sheet 1 on the right at x=55-94%, y=34-49%; sheet 2 on the left at x=6-45%, y=49-64%; sheet 3 on the right at x=55-94%, y=64-79%; sheet 4 on the left at x=6-45%, y=79-94%. All four sheets nearly rectangular, horizontal 2.7:1, straight with at most one degree natural rotation, subtle real worn fibers and occasional slightly folded corners, softly luminous cream interiors completely EMPTY for HTML text added later. Never place decorative props inside any sheet.
Leave upper-left x=5-55%, y=3-29% clean dark negative space for a large white heading. Leave x=60-95%, y=3-26% unobstructed dark negative space where the actual participant photo will be inserted in the website. Place a beautiful small antique brass mechanical stopwatch close to x=89%, y=29%, photoreal detailed metal patina, round face, fine black clock hands; not a wristwatch and absolutely no rectangular ticket. Stopwatch is about 12% canvas width, not huge, and must not obscure sheet 1.
Fine rust-copper thread runs gently between the cards in an elegant diagonal sequence, with small metal pins at the outside corners only; avoid board/corkboard. Outside cards: subtle traces of a vintage city map at low contrast, deep walnut and black matte desk texture, one tucked folded document corner near bottom-right, one fountain pen far left at bottom. Realistic paper shadows give visual depth. A warm amber glow from upper-right, edges fade into almost black #0c0908 so the asset blends into a website.
Constraints: ONE finished flat artwork, no browser chrome, no UI buttons, no labels, no numbers on sheets, NO TEXT ANYWHERE, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no faces, no people, no repeated stock collage, no torn diagonal banner, no heavy halftone, no bright sepia fog, no neon, no orange rectangular badges, no flat CSS-looking rounded rectangles. Every blank text area must be impeccably readable when dark text is overlaid. Deliver sophisticated cinematic material realism.
