Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic cover illustration for a Russian browser detective game's case card, landscape 3:2, high-resolution, no website mockup. Primary request: a sophisticated noir detective cover for the case titled 'Ставка на молчание'; the title will be typeset by the website, DO NOT place any text in this image. Warm copper and dim amber light across the paper edges, near-black brown and muted charcoal shadows, a small cool grey reflection in the window, refined cinematic realism with painterly atmospheric texture. Strong clear silhouette and editorial composition readable at 340px card size. Keep essential subjects in the central 75% to support a 3:2 crop. No people, no weapons, no blood, no roulette, no playing cards, no banknotes, no plot-revealing clues, no labels, no logos, no typography, no watermark. Full bleed, no border.

Промпт:

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: branded cover illustration for upcoming cases of a Russian detective browser game, landscape 3:2, high-resolution, not a website mockup. Primary request: an elegant mysterious detective dossier with the brand name printed clearly as the main focal point. A single dark textured closed file folder resting on an almost black warm brown background; subtle amber/copper edging, restrained film grain, low-key studio lighting, sophisticated noir atmosphere. Exact text printed once in large, extremely legible warm ivory serif lettering centered on the folder: "Детектум". Spell exactly in Cyrillic: Д е т е к т у м; capital Д and remaining lowercase. Text must fit in one line and remain readable as a small website cover. Centered clean composition, keep text in central 60% of image with generous safe space. Decorative art can include a faint concentric archival seal shape but no extra words, no subtitles, no dates, no other letters, no case title. No people, no fabricated plot clues, no badges, no watermark, no border. Warm charcoal, black coffee brown, subtle copper and ivory palette matching a premium warm noir website. Full bleed.
